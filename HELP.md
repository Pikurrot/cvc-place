# cvc-place — Help

## What this is

**cvc-place** is a shared canvas. You can upload images, place them on the map, draw, and collaborate through **contributions** (others can help fund placing an image).

## Account

- **Register** with a username, password, and **real name** (must match how you appear on the WhoIsIn list).
- New accounts will need **admin approval** before you can start participating in the canvas.

## Points

- **Starting balance** is 50 points.
- **Placing an image** costs points based on its **size on the canvas**. The app will show you the cost before you confirm.
- **You earn points by working at CVC**. Assuming your real name entered during registration matches in the WhoIsIn list, every 15 minutes you are inside CVC, you will earn 1 point.

## Contributions

Users can contribute to the canvas by funding the placement of an image. When a user creates a **contribution**, they upload an image and set a **target number of points**. The higher the target, the larger the image will be when placed. **All users** can contribute to achieve this target by donating points. Once reached the target, any user can place the image on the canvas.
